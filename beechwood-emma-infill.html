Development approach

Identify viable vacant sites; complete title, zoning and environmental due diligence; conduct resident engagement; refine building designs and cost estimates; confirm subsidy eligibility; obtain approvals; procure construction; and establish compliant leasing and long-term property management.

Neighborhood-centered outcomes

Context-sensitive infill, family-sized rental options, mixed-income opportunities, energy efficiency, accessible paths, long-term affordability and investment without displacement.

Concept informed by the published mixed-income housing and neighborhood stabilization priorities of Connected Communities, but is not affiliated with or prepared for that organization.
